Add the Object Page Anchor Bar. `ObjectPage` gets `sectionNavigation?: 'anchors' | 'none'` (default `'anchors'`) and `labels?: { anchorBar?: string }` (the name of the `<nav>`, default "Sezioni"). With two or more sections it renders, as the first child of the scroller, a sticky `<nav>` with one `<a href="#id">` per section in DOM order; it publishes its measured height as `--rop-object-page-sticky-offset` (the sections' `scroll-margin-top`). A plain click scrolls the page's own scroller (smooth, instant under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`), does not touch the URL or the history, and moves the focus to the section's title (now `tabIndex=-1`); modified clicks keep their default. Scroll-spy: the active link (`aria-current="location"`) is the last section whose top has passed the bar's bottom edge, or the last section at the end of the scroller; after a click the clicked link stays active, with no flicker through the intermediate sections, until the user scrolls by hand. The spy reads layout at most once per animation frame. The bar scrolls horizontally on its own on a narrow screen, keeps the active link in view and truncates long titles; its level is the sticky level of the z-index scale.

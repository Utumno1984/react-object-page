New `ListReportFilterBar`, the filter bar of the List Report, wired to `useListReport` through `table`. The single-choice filters are a `radiogroup` of `role="radio"` pills with a roving tab stop (the checked pill): arrows move the focus and select with wrap-around, Home/End jump to the ends, Right/Left swap in `dir="rtl"`, long labels are truncated. The search box is a `Field` + `Input` (`type="search"`) bound to `searchDraft`: Enter commits at once without submitting an enclosing form (and not during an IME composition), otherwise `searchCommitDelay` does; the clear button and Escape empty draft and committed query together and keep the focus in the input; Escape is consumed only when it clears something, so a Dialog around the bar does not close; pasted line breaks become spaces. It keeps clear of the on-screen keyboard through the Field. Below 640px the search takes the whole row and the pills wrap. Props: `table`, `filtersLabel`, `searchLabel`, `searchPlaceholder`, `clearSearchLabel`, `showSearch`, plus the container's HTML props (the bar is a `search` landmark). `Field` gains `hideLabel` (the label stays as the accessible name, visually hidden). Bundle budget: new `ListReportFilterBar` entry; the other entries moved up by about 55 B gzip, the cost of the extra module's import statements in the per-export measurement.

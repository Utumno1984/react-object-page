`StatusBadge`: record status label (tone `neutral`/`info`/`success`/`warning`/`error` + text), inline, static (no live role), never taller than a `body` text line, truncating long text with an ellipsis. Optional decorative `icon`. Colours come from the existing tokens (dark text on the tone at 10% opacity, above 4.5:1 for every tone).
